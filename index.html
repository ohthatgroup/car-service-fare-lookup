Details

Borough Park prices are 7am–11pm and don't include tolls. Late night (11pm–3am) and before 7am add 10% per hour. Waiting time is $40/hr, with free minutes by fare size. Hourly: Brooklyn car $45 / mini van $55 (30 min minimum).

Some fares were printed differently on different sheets (for example Monroe–JFK appears as both $195 and $225). The base sheet's price is the one used here.

Prices anyone adds go live right away. Changing a price takes a suggestion plus 10 votes from different people — the old price stays until the votes are in.
