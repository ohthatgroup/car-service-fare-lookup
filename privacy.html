Privacy Policy

Fare Lookup — last updated November 23, 2026

Fare Lookup is a free community car service price list. This policy explains what data the app collects and what it does with it.

What we collect

  • Sign-in (optional). You can sign in with Google to sync your trip calculator settings and saved trips across your devices. If you do, we store your Google account ID and email address for that purpose only. The calculator works fully without signing in; without sign-in, your settings and trips stay only in your browser.
  • Votes and price proposals. When you vote on a price change or propose a price, we store an anonymous browser ID with your vote, so one browser cannot vote twice.
  • Shared fare links. A shared link stores the fare amount, route, and options so the link can show the price. A shared calculator link shows only the amount and the route — never your rates or take-home.
  • Fare data. The price list itself is public community data.

Who else handles your data

Your data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database, and sign-in is handled by Google. We do not run ads and we do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

To delete your saved calculator data, ask through the Fare Lookup repository on GitHub.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Fare Lookup repository on GitHub.
